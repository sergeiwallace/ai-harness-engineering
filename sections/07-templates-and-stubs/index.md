# Templates, prompt stubs and injected prompts

As of 2026-11-09.

Stop 4 on the talk path, about 1.5 minutes.

- Agents write most of a harness's prompts, so templates for briefs and documents keep those prompts consistent.
- A sub-agent sees only its brief, so the rules it must follow go into the template.
- The harness can also send text into a live session at the right moment.
