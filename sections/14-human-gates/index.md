# Human gates, decisions and alignment

As of 2026-11-09.

Stop 10 on the talk path, about 3 minutes.

- Decide in advance which actions need a person, and let agents decide the rest with a record.
- Asking the person too often is also a failure.
- Keep the person's original words, and check each result against them.
- `/permissions` with auto mode sets allow, ask and deny rules; auto mode lets a classifier approve routine actions in long runs.
