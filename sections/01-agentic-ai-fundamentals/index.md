# Agentic AI fundamentals

As of 2026-11-09.

Stop 2 on the talk path, about 2 minutes.

- The agent loop: context, tool call, result, repeat until done.
- The client runs the tools; the model asks for them.
- The context window has a fixed size, and nothing carries between sessions unless it is written to a file.
